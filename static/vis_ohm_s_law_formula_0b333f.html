Ohm's Law (V = I · R)

Current is directly proportional to voltage and inversely proportional to resistance.

$$V = I \cdot R \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad I = \frac{V}{R} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad R = \frac{V}{I}$$

Variable Breakdown & Units

V Voltage Volts (V)
Electric potential difference
I Current Amperes (A)
Rate of electric charge flow
R Resistance Ohms (Ω)
Opposition to current flow

Derivation & Step-by-Step Logic

Proportionality
$$I \propto V$$
Current is proportional to voltage.
Equation Form
$$V = I \cdot R$$
Introduce resistance R.
Practical Worked Example:
For V = 12V and R = 4Ω: I = V / R = 12 / 4 = 3 Amperes.
